## 5b. Video level: the held-out green crab in the demo window (`scripts/rehearse_demo.py`)

Demo window 1280–1355 s (21:20–22:35, demo half). A large European green crab, which is held out of the registry, walks along the pole from about 21:31 to 21:45 (checked by eye in the frames).

* **Before approval:** the crab's track is review item #1 (the most unfamiliar), status **unfamiliar**, best similarity 0.47 to the only registry species. It is **flagged as unfamiliar, not mislabelled.** A second crab track (21:43–21:48) is also flagged unfamiliar (0.53).
* **Approved** as 'european_green_crab' from the first item's 5 crops. The re-run took 84 s from cache.
* **After approval:** 10 tracks are recognised as european_green_crab. **2 of them fall inside the walking crab's time span**, including the second, unreviewed crab track, which is now recognised automatically. The other 8 are sand or caustic patches that resemble the sandy background of the crab crops. One of them sits where the biologists circled a camouflaged crab at 22:00, but its crops can't confirm that. 17 more tracks become 'probably european_green_crab?'.
* **Conclusion:** the flag-approve-recognise loop works, but a species approved from a single track has a loose prototype. On sandy footage it over-matches until reviewers confirm more examples (Existing species) and reject sand items. Tighter crops were tested (padding 0.6 → 0.15) and improved separation only marginally (sand max 0.86 → 0.87 vs crab 0.83 → 0.85).

## 6. Honest assessment

* **Detection is the weak link.** The fish are faint, translucent and about 50 px long at 1080p. The motion detector under-detects dense, slow schools and picks up some sunlight caustics on the sand. Many of those false positives are dropped as transient, but some persist and appear as 'uncertain' review items (a reviewer rejects them in one click).
* **Species identification is limited by the references.** The only fish references are screenshot crops of one unidentified school at another station (T2-STAT2). The 'known' species is therefore 'small schooling fish (species unconfirmed)'. BioCLIP zero-shot could not name the species reliably (see RESOURCES.md).
* The image-level flag test uses screenshot crops, which come from a different domain than live video crops. On video, the review queue is the safety net.
* Every result comes from one video and two manually counted frames. Results on other sites or seasons are unknown.
